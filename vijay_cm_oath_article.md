# Historic Victory: Actor Vijay to Take Oath as Tamil Nadu's New Chief Minister

**Nehru Stadium ceremony set for May 10, 2026, marking watershed moment in state politics**

*Chennai, May 9, 2026* — Tamil Nadu is poised for a historic political transition as actor-turned-politician C. Joseph Vijay will be sworn in as Chief Minister tomorrow morning, ushering in a new era after the Dravidian parties' 59-year reign over the state's governance.

## Oath Ceremony to Begin at 10 AM

The swearing-in ceremony is scheduled to take place at Nehru Stadium in Chennai at 10:00 AM on May 10, 2026. The grand event is expected to draw massive public attention and will be attended by coalition partners and political leaders from across the country.

Vijay's Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), the actor's newly-formed political party, emerged as a revolutionary force in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly elections, winning 108 seats in the 234-member Assembly and securing the single-largest party status.

## Coalition Pact Ensures Stable Government

On May 9, Vijay met with Tamil Nadu Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar at Lok Bhavan and submitted formal confirmation of his election as Legislature Party leader. Crucially, he presented letters of support from multiple coalition partners, ensuring a stable government formation:

- Indian National Congress
- Communist Party of India (CPI)
- Communist Party of India - Marxist (CPI-M)
- Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi
- Indian Union Muslim League

This broad-based coalition demonstrates widespread support for the TVK-led government beyond its core electorate, signifying a significant realignment in Tamil Nadu's political landscape.

## Constitutional Procedures Underway

Following the submission of documents, Governor Rajendra Arlekar formally appointed Vijay as Chief Minister-designate and invited him to form the Ministry. As per constitutional protocols, Vijay has been directed to seek a vote of confidence in the Assembly on or before May 13, 2026, to formally validate his government's majority.

## Breaking a 59-Year Tradition

The oath ceremony marks a watershed moment in Tamil Nadu's political history. Vijay will become the first non-Dravidian leader to hold the position of Chief Minister in the past 59 years, signaling a seismic shift in the state's political dynamics. 

The DMK and AIADMK, which have alternated power and dominated Tamil Nadu politics since independence, have been replaced by a new political force in the form of TVK, demonstrating the electorate's desire for fresh governance and change.

## From Screen to Politics

Vijay's political journey from cinema to statecraft has captivated millions of Tamils. His party's manifesto, emphasizing welfare schemes and populist governance measures, resonated strongly with voters across urban and rural areas. The TVK's campaign focused on issues affecting the common man, contrasting with traditional political narratives.

## Expectations and Challenges Ahead

Political observers and analysts suggest that Vijay's Cabinet composition will be closely watched, as he will need to balance coalition commitments with his vision for state governance. The oath ceremony is widely expected to be symbolic of generational change and a new governance approach for Tamil Nadu.

The incoming Chief Minister faces several challenges, including economic development, welfare scheme implementation, and maintaining coalition harmony. Political experts believe his decisions in the coming weeks will set the tone for his tenure.

## State Transitions Leadership

This political transition comes after M. K. Stalin, the incumbent Chief Minister and DMK leader, stepped down following his party's electoral defeat. Stalin lost his own seat in Kolathur to TVK candidate V. S. Babu, becoming the first incumbent Chief Minister to lose assembly elections in the state since Jayalalithaa in 1996.

## A New Chapter Begins

As Tamil Nadu prepares for tomorrow's historic ceremony, the state stands at the cusp of significant political and administrative change. Vijay's oath-taking will not only mark his personal political triumph but also represent the beginning of a new chapter in the state's governance, policy-making, and political discourse.

The Nehru Stadium ceremony is expected to be a grand spectacle, with preparations underway to ensure a seamless transition of power. From the iconic actor's last film release to his first day as the Chief Minister of India's sixth-most populous state, Vijay's journey exemplifies the dynamism of Indian democracy.
