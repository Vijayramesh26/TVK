# From Fan Movement to Political Power: The Rise of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam

**TVK's remarkable ascent from a fledgling party to Tamil Nadu's ruling force in just two years reshapes state politics**

*May 10, 2026* — In a stunning political metamorphosis that defied conventional wisdom, the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), a political party barely two years old, is poised to form the government in Tamil Nadu today, ending nearly six decades of Dravidian party hegemony. Actor-politician Vijay will be sworn in as Chief Minister at Nehru Stadium at 10:00 AM, marking an unprecedented achievement in Indian electoral politics.

## From Fanclubs to Political Movement

The story of TVK does not begin with political ambitions but with fan devotion. In July 2009, actor Vijay organised his fan clubs—reportedly numbering around 85,000 across Tamil Nadu, under the umbrella of a welfare association titled Vijay Makkal Iyakkam (Vijay's People Movement).

What started as an informal gathering of movie enthusiasts gradually evolved into something more substantial. The association supported the AIADMK-led Alliance in the 2011 elections for the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly. After a period of non-involvement in politics, the organisation contested the local body elections in Tamil Nadu in 2021 and won 115 out of the 169 seats it contested.

This initial victory in local body elections proved to be a crucial turning point, demonstrating the organizational strength and electoral appeal that Vijay Makkal Iyakkam commanded among Tamil Nadu's voters.

## The Official Birth: February 2, 2024

The transformation from a welfare association to a full-fledged political party came on February 2, 2024. Vijay announced the launch of his political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), and stated his intent to contest the 2026 Tamil Nadu assembly elections.

The party is headquartered in Panaiyur, Chennai, and its ideology is rooted in secularism, social justice, and communal harmony. The party's name itself—translating to "Victory Party of Tamilakam"—conveyed Vijay's confidence and determination to contest for power.

## Ideological Foundation Takes Shape

In the months following its formal launch, TVK began articulating a clear ideological framework. In September 2024, TVK announced its ideological alignment with centre-left, following the ideologies of Ambedkar, Periyar, and Kamaraj. The party further rejected any association with right-wing politics.

This ideological positioning was crucial. By anchoring itself in Tamil Nadu's progressive political traditions while explicitly rejecting right-wing politics, TVK sought to position itself as a bridge between modernization and Tamil Nadu's historical political identity.

## The Vikravandi Conference: Proving Ground

The party's first major public test came with the Vikravandi political conference. On 27 October 2024, TVK held its first political conference in Vikravandi, which was reportedly attended by over 800,000 people.

This massive gathering was a turning point in perceptions about TVK's organizational capacity. During the conference, Vijay unveiled the party's ideology as "secular social justice" and termed the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) as an "ideological opponent" due to its right-wing politics and the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) as a "political adversary" due to its alleged corruption and dynastic politics.

The conference signaled TVK's intention to challenge not just the traditional Tamil Nadu duopoly of DMK and AIADMK but also national forces it viewed as ideologically incompatible with Tamil Nadu's ethos.

## Building Ground Organization

Following the Vikravandi conference, TVK embarked on systematic organizational building. On 13 February 2025, the party announced a large-scale enrollment drive with plans to appoint over 70,000 booth agents and restructure its internal hierarchy in preparation for the 2026 state elections.

This grassroots mobilization was complemented by strong digital campaigns and relentless focus on youth engagement. Political critics and journalists stated that anti-incumbency against the DMK government, Vijay's mobilization of his fanclubs into a unified party, and TVK's strong digital campaign portraying itself as a fresh, corruption-free alternative fueled Vijay's success.

## The Election Challenge and Solo Contest

As the 2026 election cycle approached, TVK faced a critical decision. On 18 March 2026, Vijay announced that the TVK will contest solo in all the 234 constituencies. This decision to fight the election alone, without alliance partners, was audacious and risky. It suggested complete confidence in the party's electoral appeal and resonance with voters.

The solo contest strategy proved to be astute political calculation.

## Historic Electoral Victory: May 4, 2026

The 2026 Tamil Nadu assembly elections, held on April 23, 2026, delivered a verdict that shocked political establishment. Results announced on May 4, 2026, revealed a seismic shift in state politics.

The TVK emerged as the single largest party in its first-ever election and ended a 59-year streak of dominance of Dravidian parties in the state. The election recorded the highest voter turnout in the state's history (85.1%).

TVK won 108 seats out of the 234 in the assembly, falling short of the 118-seat majority mark but positioning itself as the clear people's choice. Political critics and journalists reported that TVK hauled the votebanks of both the DMK and AIADMK, pulling their youth, women, urban, and first-time voters irrespective of caste or religious affiliations.

## The Coalition Arithmetic and Government Formation

Despite winning fewer seats than required for a simple majority, TVK's political appeal transcended caste and regional divisions. Within days of the election results, coalition partners lined up to support Vijay's government.

On 8 May, the Communist Party of India (CPI) and Communist Party of India (Marxist) (CPI(M)) agreed to support TVK for a stable, NDA-free government, while continuing their SPA membership.

Additional support came from the Indian National Congress, Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi, and the Indian Union Muslim League, building a broad secular coalition that rejected both incumbent DMK and the alliance's previous structure.

## Challenges and Controversies

The party's rapid rise was not without controversy. On 27 September 2025, during a campaign rally hosted by Vijay in the outskirts of Karur, a crowd crush resulted in the deaths of at least 41 people and left 80 others injured. Vijay shared a statement on social media expressing his condolences and announced that he intended to pay the families of the deceased ₹2 million.

The tragedy cast a shadow on the campaign but did not derail TVK's electoral momentum.

## From Cinema to Statecraft

What makes TVK's rise particularly remarkable is that it mirrors the earlier political journeys of actors M. G. Ramachandran and J. Jayalalithaa, who used their mass appeal to build political empires in Tamil Nadu. He reiterated his promise for the safety of women, welfare of youth, elderly and trans persons, farmers, labourers, weavers, fisherfolk.

Like his predecessors, Vijay leveraged his cinematic fame and appeal to transcend traditional political divisions. However, TVK's emergence also suggests something deeper: a genuine yearning for political alternatives among Tamil Nadu's electorate, particularly among younger voters fatigued with decades of alternating DMK and AIADMK rule.

## The Road Ahead

As Vijay prepares to take oath today, TVK faces the formidable challenge of translating electoral victory into effective governance. The party must now deliver on its promises of corruption-free administration, welfare schemes, and inclusive development while managing a diverse coalition government.

The Madurai conference in August 2025 had outlined TVK's governance vision, but execution remains the true test. On 26 April 2025, the party held a meeting of the party functionaries in Coimbatore, where Vijay stated his commitment to establish a corruption-free, transparent, and accountable government.

## A New Chapter for Tamil Nadu

The rise of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam represents a watershed moment not just for Tamil Nadu but for Indian democracy. A regional party without any previous electoral experience has managed to become the ruling party, demonstrating the unpredictability and vitality of Indian electoral democracy.

From Vijay Makkal Iyakkam's fan clubs to TVK's governance mandate is a journey that defied political convention and expert predictions. As the actor-turned-politician takes the oath as Chief Minister today, Tamil Nadu embarks on a new political journey—one where cinema and politics have merged to reshape state administration.

The next five years will determine whether TVK can sustain this momentum and fulfill its promise to deliver a new kind of politics to Tamil Nadu, one that prioritizes people-first leadership and corruption-free governance over dynastic politics.
